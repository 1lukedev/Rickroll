Every year, a team of passionate web designers, developers, and digital creators at an agency come together to create a fun, unexpected website just for the internet to enjoy.

To bring this year’s project to life, 66 members of their team voluntarily donated £1 each toward the cause. That pooled fund covered the domain name, server setup, and hosting costs for this ad-free experience at rickrolling.co.uk.
